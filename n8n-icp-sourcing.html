Automated ICP Sourcing from HubSpot Won & Lost Deals

How it works: Extracts historical Closed-Won and Closed-Lost deals from HubSpot CRM, resolves all associated Company and Contact IDs via HubSpot's v4 Batch Associations API, chunks records into batches of 100 for high-speed batch reading, builds comparative training rows in JavaScript, and uses an OpenAI Chat Model + Structured Output Parser to synthesize a data-backed Ideal Customer Profile (ICP) JSON payload.

1. Why Is This Important?

Replaces Guesswork with CRM Win/Loss Data

Most GTM teams define their Ideal Customer Profile (ICP) based on gut feeling or only look at Won deals—ignoring Lost deals that reveal negative disqualifiers. Contrasting both Won and Lost deal cohorts uncovers what actually drives conversion.

2. What Value Does It Bring?

100-Record Batching + Structured AI Schema

Instead of making thousands of single HubSpot API calls that hit rate limits, this workflow chunks Company and Contact IDs into batches of 100 and feeds aggregated firmographic and persona signals into OpenAI with a strict JSON schema parser so parent workflows can consume the ICP directly.

3. How Much Time Does It Save?

~15–20 Hours of RevOps Analysis per Run

Exporting deals, cross-referencing associated companies and buyer titles across HubSpot objects, and running win/loss statistical breakdowns manually takes days of RevOps analyst time. This sub-workflow executes the entire pipeline in ~20 seconds.
