Step 2 • HubSpot Enrollment & 30-Day Cooling Filter
Contacted < 30 Days Ago

Remove from Table

Drops recently contacted prospects completely to prevent outreach collision.

Not Found in HubSpot

Classified as NOT ENROLLED (Net-New Lead). Placed at top priority.

Step 3 • Priority Sorting & Ranking

Surface Freshest Opportunities First

Places all ENROLLED IN HS = No (net-new leads) at the top of the table, followed by ENROLLED IN HS = Yes sorted ascending by Last Contacted Date (oldest contacted first).

Sort Rule: Net-New first → Earliest Last Contacted → Most Recently Contacted

Passes the filtered, ranked list back through Apollo Enrichment to populate primary verified Email, Location (City, Country), Seniority, Job Title, Company Domain, and LinkedIn URL.

Guardrail: Leave unavailable fields blank; never fabricate data
