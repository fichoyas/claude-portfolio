Trade Show Exhibitor Website Scraper & Apollo Enricher

How it works: Scrapes live trade show and conference exhibitor directories, crawls each individual exhibitor's profile page to extract company names and URLs, normalizes domains in JavaScript, and routes every company through a dual-path Apollo Organization Search (searching by exact Domain when available, or falling back to Company Name when no website was listed on the event page) before exporting a clean CSV.

📊 Workflow Impact & Business Value Automated Event & Directory Lead Capture
1. Why Is This Important?

Turns Unstructured Event Pages into Pipeline

Trade show exhibitor lists are goldmines of high-intent industry accounts, but event organizers lock them inside paginated HTML directories without downloadable CSVs or clean company domains—forcing SDRs to click into hundreds of sub-pages manually.

2. What Value Does It Bring?

Dual-Fallback Domain & Name Resolution

Many exhibitor profiles omit their website URL or include messy booth text. By cleaning scraped text in JavaScript and routing companies through an If gate (Apollo Domain Search when a URL exists, or Apollo Name Search when missing), zero exhibitors are lost.

3. How Much Time Does It Save?

~6–8 Hours Saved per Trade Show

Manually clicking through 300+ exhibitor booth pages, copying company names and URLs, and enriching firmographics in Apollo takes a full workday per conference. This scraper extracts, enriches, and exports the entire exhibitor list in under 2 minutes.
