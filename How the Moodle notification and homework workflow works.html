How the Moodle notification and homework workflow works

Twice a week the workflow checks Moodle for anything new, saves new documents to your computer and messages you on WhatsApp. If you answer "run", a second skill called Homework master files the material by course and prepares the work or a study summary. Each numbered step below does one job, and what you receive is at the bottom.

Start: the clock and one script

Nothing needs to be uploaded. The workflow starts on its own every Monday and Friday at 15:00.

Moodle connection script

A Python script holding the Moodle and WhatsApp connection details. Those details are not shown here.

Purpose description

A context file that explains what the workflow is for.

Course Content depository

Your collection of course material, one folder per course.

Your WhatsApp number

Where every message is sent, and where your "run" or "no" answer comes from.

1

Wake up and read Moodle

Asks Moodle and runs a script

Every Monday and Friday at 15:00 the workflow starts by itself. A script connects to Moodle, the university learning platform, and reads every folder of every course you are enrolled in.

Result: a complete picture of what is on Moodle right now.

1a. When it runs

Automatically, twice a week. You do not have to start it.

1b. What it reads

All folders of all available courses: lecture slides, exercises, announcements and new sections.

1c. How it connects

Through the Moodle connection stored in the attached script. The login details stay in the script and are not shown on this page.

2

Spot what is new

Compares dates and decides

Checks the upload date of every item against the date of the previous run. Only items uploaded after that date count as new.

Result: a short list of new documents, new sections and new announcements.

2a. New documents

Files uploaded after the last run.

2b. New sections

Course sections that did not exist at the last run.

2c. New announcements

News posted in a course Announcements forum after the last run.

Nothing new

The run ends quietly. You are not messaged and nothing is downloaded.

3

Save the new documents to your computer

Works on the files only

Downloads every newly attached document to local disk D, so you always have your own copy.

Result: the new files, saved on disk D.

3a. What is saved

Only documents that are new since the last run, not everything again.

4

Tell you about announcements

Messages you on WhatsApp

If a course posted news in its Announcements, the workflow sends a WhatsApp message to your number straight away.

Result: a WhatsApp message with the announcement news.

4a. When it is sent

Only when there is at least one new announcement.

4b. What it says

Which course posted the news and what the news is, in a short message.

5

Summarise the new material and ask you

Messages you on WhatsApp

If there are new documents or sections, the workflow writes a short summary of what was uploaded, sends it to your WhatsApp and asks what to do next.

Result: a WhatsApp message with the summary and a question: "run" or "no".

5a. The summary

Which course, which new documents or sections, in plain language.

5b. The question

You decide: reply run to have the new material processed, or no to skip it.

Waiting for you

Nothing further happens until you reply. You stay in control.

6

Your answer decides

Works on the files only

Reads your WhatsApp reply and follows one of two paths.

Result: either the next step starts, or the run is logged and closed.

6a. You reply "run"

The workflow hands over to the Homework master skill (steps 7 and 8).

6b. You reply "no"

The workflow writes two dates into its log: the upload date of the new materials on the Moodle page, and the date of this workflow run. That run date is what the next run compares against.

7

File the materials by course

Works on the files only

Only after "run". Uploads the new materials into the data depository called Course Content and puts each one in the folder named after the course that published it.

Result: the new materials sit in the right course folder in Course Content.

7a. Matching

The folder whose name contains the course name receives the material.

7b. Why

Homework master can then use everything you have collected for a course, not only the newest file.

8

Homework master does the work

Uses the Homework master skill

A second skill reads what was sent, looks into Course Content and completes what the new materials ask for. If nothing is asked, it prepares a study summary instead.

Result: finished work or an in-depth summary, plus a list of things to know before the week.

8a. Understand the job

Reads the information handed over by the workflow and works out what is needed.

8b. Open Course Content

Calls the data depository so it can use all material collected so far.

8c. There is a specific task

It completes the task described in the new materials, using everything available in Course Content.

8d. There is no specific task

It reads the new lecture or exercise content, writes an in-depth summary and sends it to your WhatsApp.

8e. Before the new week

Finishes with bullet points: anything you need to know before the new week and the next classes begin.

End

Output: messages, saved files and a log

You get WhatsApp messages and saved files, not a report: news alerts, a summary of new material with a question, and after "run" the finished work or an in-depth summary. New documents are on disk D and filed by course.
