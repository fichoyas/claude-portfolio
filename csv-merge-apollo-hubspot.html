CSV Merge → Apollo Enrichment → HubSpot Check

Overview: An auditable, read-only data pipeline that loads multiple prospect CSVs, normalizes schema variants and legal suffixes, deduplicates companies and contacts using a multi-tier confidence hierarchy, enriches records via Apollo.io, verifies HubSpot existence and sequence enrollment, and outputs a CRM-ready dataset with safety recommendations.

Connectors & Guardrails: 📂 /mnt/user-data/uploads/ CSVs 🚀 Apollo.io Connector (Read-Only) 🟠 HubSpot CRM Connector (Read-Only)
Step 1 • Load & Inspect CSVs

Parse Files & Detect Identifiers

Reads uploaded CSVs from /mnt/user-data/uploads/. Detects delimiters, encoding, row counts, and company/contact keys. Halts and requests a corrected file if unparseable.

Company Keys: domain, website, LinkedIn URL, company name
Contact Keys: email, LinkedIn URL, full name
Steps 2 & 3 • Schema & Value Normalization

Canonical Field Mapping & Cleaning

Maps raw headers to canonical fields (preserving overwritten data as original_*). Lowercases domains/emails, strips URL tracking params, and normalizes legal suffixes (GmbH, Ltd, AG, Inc) for matching only.

Canonical Map: company_name, company_domain, contact_email, contact_title
LinkedIn Logic: Infers person vs. company by URL pattern
Step 4 • Append & Hierarchical Deduplication
High-Confidence Match

Merge & Assign Dedupe Key

Matches via Company Order (Domain → Website → LinkedIn → Name+Country) and Contact Order (Email → LinkedIn → Name+Domain).

Lineage: Tracks source_file, source_row_id, dedupe_key, merge_status
Ambiguous Candidate

Do Not Merge (Safety Flag)

Preserves separate rows if any chance exists they are distinct entities. Flags ambiguous_duplicate.

Guardrail: Never merge on company name alone if conflicts exist
🚀 Step 5 • Apollo.io Enrichment Engine Confidence: high | medium | low | none
Clear Winner Found

Populate 13 Apollo Fields

Enriches company ID, domain, industry, employee count, revenue, country, contact email, title, and LinkedIn URL.

Lookup Order: Domain → Website → LinkedIn → Name+Country
Multiple Candidates / Rate Limit

Flag Ambiguous & Leave Blank

Sets apollo_enrichment_status = "ambiguous" and recommends manual Apollo review. Never fabricates data.

Fallback: If Apollo offline, outputs Apollo-ready deduplicated file
Step 6 • HubSpot Existence & Enrollment Check

Verify CRM Records, Deals & Sequence Status

Checks if Company/Contact exists in HubSpot, retrieves Deal IDs, Lifecycle Stage, Owner, and Last Activity Date, and classifies hubspot_enrollment_status.

Statuses: confirmed_enrolled | likely_enrolled | existing_not_enrolled | not_found | ambiguous
Audit: Explicitly documents which fields proved enrollment
Steps 7 & 8 • CRM Action Router & Quality Flags
Already Enrolled / Owned
skip_existing_in_hubspot

High-confidence match already active in sequences or owned.

Unclear / Missing Keys
review / manual_review

Ambiguous match, unclear enrollment, or missing identifiers.

Verified Net-New
safe_to_import_or_enroll

No HubSpot collision and complete identifier quality.

✅ Step 9 • Final Defined Output Master CSV + Chat Audit
  • Auditable Master CSV: Normalized source columns + 13 Apollo enrichment columns + 11 HubSpot CRM columns + dedupe_key, recommended_action, and semicolon-separated data_quality_flags.
  • Executive Pipeline Summary: Breakdown of input row counts, deduplicated totals, Apollo enrichment hit rate, HubSpot enrollment counts, and top data-quality issues.
