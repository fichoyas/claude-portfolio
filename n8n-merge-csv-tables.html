Merge & Deduplicate Apollo + ZoomInfo CSV Tables

How it works: Ingests two different prospect exports (Apollo CSV and ZoomInfo CSV) via a single form upload, unifies their disparate column schemas, runs a two-stage deduplication pipeline (first by normalized First + Last Name, and finally by Email after backfilling missing emails via the Apollo People Enrichment API), and outputs a single clean master CSV.

🔍 Zoom Controls: 100%
📊 Workflow Impact & Business Value Cross-Platform List Hygiene
1. Why Is This Important?

Prevents Cross-Provider Duplicate Outreach

When GTM teams source leads from both Apollo and ZoomInfo, the same prospect frequently appears in both exports—sometimes with a missing email in one tool or a secondary email in the other. Merging without two-stage deduplication causes embarrassing double-outreach.

2. What Value Does It Bring?

Two-Stage Dedupe + Automatic Email Backfill

Deduplicates first by First + Last Name before API calls (to save enrichment credits), automatically backfills any missing emails via Apollo's People Match endpoint, and performs a second strict deduplication pass on Email before exporting.

3. How Much Time Does It Save?

~2 Hours Saved per Campaign List

Manually aligning Apollo and ZoomInfo column headers, writing VLOOKUP/XLOOKUP formulas, enriching blank email rows, and deduplicating across two keys takes 1.5–2 hours per list. Here, uploading both files produces a clean master CSV in 15 seconds.
